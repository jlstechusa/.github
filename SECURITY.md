# Security Policy

JLS Technology USA, LLC welcomes reports of vulnerabilities in the software,
tools, datasets, and research artifacts we publish under this organization.

## Reporting

- Preferred: use GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" button on the affected
  repository (private vulnerability reporting).
- Email: security@jlstech.com
- Machine-readable contact: https://jlstech.com/.well-known/security.txt

Please include the affected repository and version or commit, steps to
reproduce, and the impact as you understand it. Do not include client data or
personal data in a report.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgment within 3 business days
- Initial assessment and severity within 10 business days
- Status updates at least every 30 days until resolved
- Credit in our acknowledgments if you want it, once resolved

We do not operate a bug bounty program.

## Scope and safe harbor

This policy covers repositories under this organization and jlstech.com. It
does not cover our clients' systems; report those to the client directly.
Good-faith research conducted under our published policy is authorized and
will not be the subject of legal action by JLS Technology USA.

Full policy, including our coordinated-disclosure practice when we report
vulnerabilities in others' software:
https://jlstech.com/vulnerability-disclosure-policy/
